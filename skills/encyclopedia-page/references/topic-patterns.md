# Section patterns by topic type

Pick the closest pattern and adapt it. Section titles below are in English; write them in the page language. Sections marked * are optional.

## Contents
- Concept (science, math, philosophy)
- Equation, derivation or homework task
- Organism or taxon
- Person
- Place
- Historical event
- Technology, product or method
- Work (book, film, artwork)

---

## Concept (science, math, philosophy)
Infobox: field, defined by, symbol and units, related concepts, illustration of the idea.
1. In one sentence (green box)
2. Intuition: everyday picture first, then the analogy
3. Definition: the precise statement; every symbol explained
4. How it works or why it is true: the mechanism, with a figure
5. Examples: 2–3 worked examples from simple to realistic
6. Orders of magnitude*: a table or calculator
7. History*
8. Applications: what it is used for
9. Common misconceptions (⚠️)
10. Glossary · References · See also

## Equation, derivation or homework task
Infobox: the equation, its source (book, chapter, page), field, approximation or assumptions, ingredients (equations it uses), consequences, difficulty.
1. The task in one sentence: what is given, what to show, and what kind of work it is (algebra, integration, limit…)
2. Table of what is data and what is unknown
3. Physical or conceptual context from the ground up, so a non-expert knows why each object exists
4. The tools: notation and rules needed, such as index tricks, identities or approximations, in a table
5. Map of the derivation: an SVG flowchart showing where this step sits in the chapter
6. The plan in N moves: strategy without answers, plus a quality-control box (units, orders, limits)
7. Results hidden in `details.spoiler`, term by term
8. Physical interpretation: what each term means; a check against a known exact result or limit
9. What comes next: how the result is used later
10. Errata (⚠️) if the source has any, with printed and corrected versions side by side
11. Glossary · References

## Organism or taxon (e.g. "ofidio" = Serpentes)
Infobox (taxobox): SVG silhouette, a "Scientific classification" band with rows for kingdom, phylum, class, order, suborder, family…, the taxon name in italics, authority and year, number of species if known, distribution.
1. Etymology: origin of the name (e.g. Greek *ophis*, "snake")
2. Evolution and classification: an SVG cladogram; main families in a table
3. Anatomy: a labelled SVG diagram of the key organs or features, explaining how each works (e.g. jaw, scales, Jacobson's organ)
4. Senses and locomotion: an SVG showing the movement types
5. Diet and feeding
6. Reproduction
7. Venom*: types, how it acts, medically important species; a myths box
8. Distribution and habitat
9. Relationship with humans: culture, myths, conservation, bites and first aid (careful, factual)
10. Myths and facts (⚠️ box)
11. Size calculator or comparison table*
12. Glossary · References · See also · Navbox (related taxa)

## Person
Infobox: portrait placeholder (an SVG monogram, never an invented likeness), born and died, nationality, fields, known for, awards.
1. Early life · 2. Career or work, as a timeline SVG · 3. Main contributions, each explained for a non-expert · 4. Personal life* · 5. Legacy · 6. Quotes* · References.
Stick to well-documented facts; for living people, avoid private details.

## Place
Infobox: SVG locator map (schematic), country or region, coordinates, area, population and year, altitude, climate.
1. Toponymy · 2. Geography (SVG map) · 3. History (timeline) · 4. Climate (SVG chart) · 5. Demographics · 6. Economy · 7. Culture · 8. Places of interest · References.

## Historical event
Infobox: date, place, participants, outcome, casualties if relevant.
1. Background (causes) · 2. Timeline (SVG) · 3. Development · 4. Key figures (table) · 5. Consequences · 6. Historiography and debates · 7. Legacy · References.

## Technology, product or method
Infobox: inventor or developer, first appeared, type, how it works in one line, schematic.
1. How it works (SVG diagram, step by step) · 2. History · 3. Variants (comparison table) · 4. Uses · 5. Limitations and risks · 6. Future · References.

## Work (book, film, artwork)
Infobox: creator, year, genre, medium, length.
1. Synopsis (no spoilers in the lead; put plot details under a spoiler) · 2. Themes · 3. Creation · 4. Reception · 5. Influence · References.
Do not reproduce copyrighted text beyond short quotations.
